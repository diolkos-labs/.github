Research and prototypes for IBC between Cardano and EVM chains.

The [Diolkos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diolkos) was a paved trackway across the
Isthmus of Corinth. From about 600 BC, boats were hauled along it between the Gulf of
Corinth and the Saronic Gulf instead of sailing around the Peloponnese. We work on the
same crossing between ledgers: moving IBC packets between Cardano and EVM chains, with
light clients on both sides.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Nothing here is a deployable bridge, and none of it holds real assets. The
> repositories are partial implementations with hash-bound test evidence; each one
> states exactly what is and is not implemented.
